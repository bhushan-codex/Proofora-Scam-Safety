1. Introduction & Core Principles

Proofora: Scam Safety ("Proofora", "the Application", "we", "our", or "us"), created by Bhushan-Codex-Studio, is an Android security utility designed to help users identify deceptive communication patterns, analyze suspicious text messages or links, and navigate defensive containment steps.

We operate under a privacy-first, on-device model:

  • Local Execution: Heuristic threat evaluation is engineered to execute directly within your device’s isolated sandbox memory.
  • No Mandatory Accounts: Core utilities do not require account registration, passwords, or personal profile creation.
  • No Monetization of User Data: We do not sell, rent, broker, or monetize your personal text inputs or evaluation records.

3. Information the App Processes

Depending on which tools you choose to use within the application, Proofora may process the following types of information:

  • Text & Link Inputs: Text snippets, URLs, telephone numbers, or payment handles that you paste or type into the inspection tool.
  • Local Check History: Risk assessment summaries, threat scores, and flagged evidence items generated during your active session.
  • User-Authored Records: Incident logs, personal security notes, and containment checklist completion states stored in the app vault.

4. Message Text & Suspicious-Content Checks

On-Device Analysis: The textual content of messages, URLs, and phone numbers submitted for screening is evaluated locally using client-side heuristics.

The application scans text for deceptive cues, such as artificial urgency, impersonation triggers, lookalike top-level domains, and known coercive language.

  • We do not upload your analyzed SMS text, clipboard data, or input snippets to developer-operated backend servers.
  • Analysis is performed in transient memory during your screening session.

5. Notes, Local History & User-Created Content

Proofora provides features such as a "Safety Vault" for saving flagged checks, logging suspicious contacts, and tracking defensive checklist tasks.

  • All saved evaluations, incident notes, and checklist progress are stored locally on your Android device (using local storage mechanisms such as SQLite/Room or app preferences).
  • This content remains on your physical hardware and is not synced to a remote cloud database operated by the developer unless explicitly requested or added in a future update.

6. Information Collected Automatically

The core application code of Proofora does not contain background tracking software or covert telemetry tools.

However, when you download, install, or run any Android app, the underlying operating system and distribution platform (such as Google Play Services and Android OS) may independently collect system-level telemetry, such as:

  • Device hardware specifications, operating system version, and general network connectivity status.
  • Aggregated, anonymized install and crash statistics generated through Google Play Console vitals.

Such diagnostic data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Device Permissions & Their Purpose

Proofora adheres to the principle of least privilege. The application does not request sensitive Android runtime permissions (such as Background Location, Contacts access, Camera, or SMS inbox reading) to execute its core screening functions.

Permission Type Purpose
INTERNET Normal / Install-time Allows users to follow external links to official government reporting portals (e.g., FTC, IdentityTheft.gov) via their default browser. Core threat screening functions operate offline without internet connectivity.

8. Cookies & Similar Tracking Technologies

The Proofora Android application does not use HTTP cookies, web beacons, tracking pixels, or third-party advertising identifiers (AD_ID).

To maintain transparency, the following technical components are documented:

  • No Third-Party Advertising SDKs: Proofora contains zero advertising networks (e.g., AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin).
  • No Third-Party Analytics SDKs: We do not integrate third-party behavioral trackers (e.g., Facebook SDK, AppsFlyer).

10. How Information Is Used

Any information processed by the application is utilized strictly for the following purposes:

  • To execute immediate client-side heuristic pattern checks on text submitted by you.
  • To display threat indicators, calculated risk severity ratings, and recommended defensive scripts.
  • To track your progress through self-directed emergency containment checklists.
  • To save your personal incident notes within your device’s local application sandbox.

11. How Information Is Stored

Proofora is designed as an offline-first utility. All user-created records (saved scans, incident logs, completed checklist states) reside inside the private internal storage directory assigned to com.bhushan.proofora by the Android OS.

Other applications on your device cannot access this sandbox under standard Android security architecture unless your device has been modified (rooted) or compromised.

12. Information Sharing, Disclosure & Sale

No Sale of Data: We do not sell, rent, disclose, or trade user data, inspected messages, or incident logs to any commercial third party, data broker, or marketing entity.

Because we do not collect or maintain your message contents on remote servers, we do not have user databases to disclose or provide to outside entities.

13. Data Retention & Deletion

You have direct, unilateral control over all data stored by Proofora:

  • Item-by-Item Deletion: You can delete any saved scan or incident record directly within the "Safety Vault" tab at any time.
  • Complete Data Wipe: Navigating to your device's Settings > Apps > Proofora > Storage > Clear Data immediately deletes all locally saved scans, incident logs, and checklist progress.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling Proofora causes the Android operating system to completely remove the application's private sandbox directory and all stored data.

14. Children’s Privacy

Proofora: Scam Safety is a general-purpose security and awareness utility. It is not directed toward children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

15. Security Safeguards & Limitations

We implement industry-standard practices, including local sandboxing and minimal permission scopes. However, please recognize the inherent limitations of mobile software:

  • No method of electronic storage or device-level operation is 100% secure against advanced malware, compromised hardware, or unauthorized physical device access.
  • We cannot protect information stored on a device that has been rooted, jailbroken, or infected with keyloggers or malicious screen recorders.

16. User Rights & Choices

Under data protection regulations (such as GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and state privacy statutes), users maintain rights regarding access, deletion, and portability. Because Proofora does not hold your data on remote servers:

  • You exercise your right to access and view your data directly within the app's local interface.
  • You exercise your right to delete your data directly via the in-app deletion actions or by clearing app data.
